## Long Description

# Kibitz — Your AI Gaming Co-Pilot

Kibitz is a Discord bot that **watches your game screen in real-time** and delivers witty, helpful reactions — from clutch callouts to strategy tips — right in your server.

## How It Works

1. Add Kibitz to your server
2. Download the free Companion App (Windows)
3. Launch your game and type `/kibitz-start`
4. Kibitz analyzes your screen using Claude AI and reacts automatically

## What Kibitz Does

- **Auto-reacts** to deaths, boss fights, victories, and discoveries
- **Answers questions mid-game** — ask "What should I build?" and get instant advice
- **Joins your voice channel** and speaks reactions aloud (Pro)
- **Knows 30+ games** including Elden Ring, VALORANT, Apex, Minecraft, Genshin Impact, League of Legends, and more
- **Adapts its personality** — tactical coach for FPS, friendly guide for RPG, hype teammate for MOBA

## Commands

- `/kibitz-start` — Start watching your game
- `/kibitz-stop` — Stop and leave voice
- `/kibitz-hint` — Get a quick tip about your current screen
- `/kibitz-analyze` — Detailed breakdown of your game state
- `/kibitz-mode` — Manually set your game
- `/kibitz-persona` — Change AI personality (FPS, RPG, MOBA, Survival, Action)
- `/kibitz-upgrade` — Learn about Pro features
